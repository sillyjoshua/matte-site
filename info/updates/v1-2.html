Matte V1.2 — Silver Gate

TAG: matte-v1.2-silvergate · THE VOICE UPDATE

Matte gets a voice

V1.2 was the update where Matte stopped being just a pretty window and started feeling alive. The headline: Jarvis — an assistant living right in the address bar.

Jarvis

  • Type "jarvis go to youtube", "jarvis open github" or "jarvis search cats" — and Matte just does it
  • A fast local command parser handles everyday commands instantly, recognizing 20+ popular sites by name — zero network, zero setup
  • For complex commands, users can connect their own AI key in Settings (opt-in, stored only on their device — Matte ships with no keys and no accounts)
  • An animated ◈ JARVIS badge pulses in the top bar while it works

The peak top bar

V1.2 threw out the old multi-row header and rebuilt it as one sleek 44-pixel strip, Chrome-style:

  • Nav buttons (including a new Home button), tabs, omnibox and window controls in a single row
  • Tabs as rounded pills — glowing active state, hover lift, per-tab loading dot, close-on-hover, middle-click close
  • A purple profile avatar, bookmark star and ⋯ menu on the right edge
  • A big, calm omnibox pill taking the remaining width

Why it mattered

V1.0 gave Matte its face. V1.1 gave it a brain. V1.2 gave it a voice — and it earned Matte its first real users in the wild. The road to V2 started here.
